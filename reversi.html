How to play

  • Black moves first. Players alternate placing one disc at a time.
  • A move is legal only if your new disc sandwiches at least one enemy disc between it and another of your discs.
  • Sandwiches may run horizontal, vertical, or diagonal. Every captured line flips to your color.
  • A single move can capture in several directions at once.
  • If you have no legal move, your turn is passed automatically.
  • The game ends when neither player can move, even if empty squares remain.
  • The player with the most discs wins. Equal totals are a draw.

Quick strategy

  • Corners are permanent once taken and are the strongest squares.
  • Be cautious beside an empty corner; those moves can hand the corner to your opponent.
  • Early on, more discs is not always better. Keeping good future moves matters more.
  • Edges become increasingly valuable, especially when anchored by a corner.
